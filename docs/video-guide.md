# Casebound demo video: recording guide

Goal: about 2:30 of screen video, recorded silently. The voice (Kokoro "Heart") is added afterwards, so just click calmly. Pauses are fine, they get trimmed.

## Before you record (5 min)

1. Open Chrome in a **normal window** (not the Claude tab group), and go to https://casebound.zycody.com
2. Wake the server: if the page takes ~50 s to load, that's normal. Wait until it shows.
3. Make sure it's unlocked: top right shows **"PayPal sandbox"** and no "Locked" link. If locked, click it and enter the passcode.
4. Reset the practice cases you'll use: open each one, click **"Reset practice case"** at the top:
   - $210 "Item not received" (Rosa Diaz)
   - $6 "Duplicate charge" (Casey Brooks)
5. Clean screen: hide the bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B), close other tabs, turn on Do Not Disturb, browser zoom at 100% or 110%.
6. Start recording: **Cmd+Shift+5** → "Record Selected Portion" → draw the box around the Chrome window → Record.

## Scenes

Move the mouse slowly. Wait ~2 seconds after every click before the next one.

### Scene 1: The inbox (≈20 s)
- Start on the **Inbox** page, at the top.
- Hover slowly over **"Money at risk"**, then **"Soonest deadline"**.
- Move down over the table rows (don't click yet). Hover the **"Quick call: refund"** text on the $6 row.

### Scene 2: The quick call, no AI (≈20 s)
- Click the **$6.00 Duplicate charge** row.
- Hover the green box **"Quick call, no AI needed: Refund it. Fighting can't pay off."** and let it be read (3 s).
- Click **"Refund $6.00"**. Wait for the confirmation.
- Click **"← Inbox"** at the top left.

### Scene 3: The big case (≈80 s, the heart of the video)
- Click the **$210.00 Item not received** row (Rosa Diaz).
- Hover the reviewer checklist on the left for 2 s (the orange "!" lines show the evidence conflict).
- Scroll back up. Click **"Investigate this dispute"**.
- **Do nothing while it runs** (about 60 s). Just let the steps appear. We speed this part up in editing.
  - The key moments: "WORKING LEAN → FIGHT", then **"CHANGED MY MIND → ACCEPT"**.
- When the verdict sheet with the **ACCEPT** stamp appears:
  - Hover the orange **"Changed my mind"** box (3 s).
  - Hover the money bars: **"Refund now $0.00"** vs **"Fight $14.75"** (3 s).
  - Move to the right panel, **"Review and send"**. Hover the message to the buyer (3 s).
  - Click **"Technical details: the exact PayPal request"** to open it, hover 2 s.
  - Click **"Approve and send"**. Wait for the confirmation.

### Scene 4: Proof it's real PayPal (≈20 s)
- Click **"Audit log"** in the top menu.
- Hover the rows that say **"PayPal accepted ✓"** (these are real PayPal sandbox calls).
- Click one dispute ID (for example **PP-R-...**), scroll to its history, and open **"Request and PayPal's reply"**. Hover 3 s.

### Scene 5: The end (≈10 s)
- Click **"Inbox"** in the top menu.
- Stay still for 5 seconds on the inbox. Stop recording (the stop button in the menu bar, top right).
